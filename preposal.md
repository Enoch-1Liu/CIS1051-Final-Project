## What idea(s) do you have for your final project?

Math equation visualizer using the manimgl library. We want to create visualizations seen in videos like those made by 3Blue1Brown.
Specifically, we want to visualize the fourier transform. We also want to create something that allows users to input their own equations, namely graphs in 2d, 3d, and matrices, if time allows.
